How Fresh came to be

  1. The idea

    One place to revise together

    Junyi started Fresh with a simple goal. IGCSE students should be able to find notes and practice in one place, and students who build their own resources should have somewhere to share them.

  2. 3 November 2026

    The idea becomes a website

    The first version gave 12 IGCSE subjects a page of their own. AI coding tools helped turn the subject outlines into a working homepage and revision pages.

  3. 5 November 2026

    Subjects grow beyond starter pages

    Jayden Tan built EcBiz for Business Studies. Business and Economics became early examples of how a subject could have its own revision tools and still be easy to find through Fresh.

  4. 18 November 2026

    School feedback shapes how progress is saved

    Business and Economics moved to browser-only progress so students could save their work without signing in. CSV downloads gave them a way to keep a copy.

  5. 24–25 November 2026

    More students bring their projects

    Chemistry and Physics reopened with updated lessons. MathQuest, Vertex Maths, PyQuest and Alexey Lykosov’s RM Revise joined the hub alongside them.

  6. 26 November 2026

    The subject projects connect

    Biology and the Literature Study Desk joined as the hub grew. One shared GIS Points balance made it possible to earn points while revising different subjects.
