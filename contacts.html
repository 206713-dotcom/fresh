About Us & Contacts

Fresh is a student-led organisation that brings Cambridge IGCSE revision together in one place.

Timeline General contacts Subject contacts

Why we built Fresh

Revision gets harder when notes, practice questions and progress are spread across different places. We built Fresh so you can pick a topic, try a question and work out what to revise next.

Students contribute resources across 12 subjects, from flashcards and worked examples to virtual practicals and exam practice. You can start without an account, and your saved progress stays in your browser.

Fresh timeline

  1. 3 November 2026

    The first study hub

    Fresh started with a homepage and 12 IGCSE subject pages.

  2. 18 November 2026

    Progress stayed in the browser

    Business and Economics moved to browser-only progress and added CSV downloads.

  3. 24–25 November 2026

    More subject apps joined

    Chemistry and Physics reopened after an update. Fresh added MathQuest, Vertex Maths, PyQuest and RM Revise.

  4. 26 November 2026

    Biology, English and shared points

    Fresh added the Biology workspace and linked the Literature Study Desk. One GIS Points balance replaced separate subject coins.

  5. 27 November 2026

    A way to reach the team

    Junyi and Lucas became the listed general contacts, with a feedback link for every subject.
