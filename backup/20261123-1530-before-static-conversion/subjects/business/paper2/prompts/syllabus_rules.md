# CAMBRIDGE IGCSE BUSINESS (0264) — 2027–2029 SYLLABUS WHITELIST & BLACKLIST
# Reference: Section 3 of the Paper 2 generation mandate.

## WHITELIST (in syllabus — permitted)
Unit 1 — Business activity & business environment
  business organisation (sole trader, partnership, private limited company, public limited company, cooperative),
  business plans, aims & objectives, stakeholders, growth (organic, takeover, merger/vertical/horizontal integration),
  sources of finance (internal: retained profit, sale of assets; external: bank loan, overdraft, share issue, trade credit, venture capital, leasing),
  dividend, limited liability, franchise, multinational, private/public sector, business environment.
Unit 2 — Human resources
  workforce planning, recruitment (internal/external, job description, person specification, CV, interview, testing, aptitude tests),
  selection, induction/on-the-job/off-the-job training, motivation theories (Maslow, Herzberg, Taylor),
  non-financial & financial motivators (bonus, commission, profit sharing, job enrichment, job rotation, team working),
  appraisal, leadership styles (autocratic, democratic, laissez-faire),
  organisational structures (flat, hierarchical, matrix? — avoid matrix), chain of command, span of control,
  communication (formal/informal, downward/upward/horizontal, barriers), employment contract, trade unions, redundancy, absenteeism, labour turnover.
Unit 3 — Finance & accounting AND operations costing
  statements of profit or loss / of financial position (as extracts), gross profit, revenue (turnover), cost of sales, operating profit,
  break-even output, contribution per unit, margin of safety, fixed/variable costs, average total cost, labour productivity,
  cash flow, budget, working capital (current assets), ratios: current ratio, acid test/liquid/quick ratio, gross profit margin, profit margin, ROCE,
  ratio analysis for decision-making, interpreting financial data.
Unit 4 — Operations, technology, sustainability & quality
  Unit 4.2 technologies: CAM (computer-aided manufacturing), automation/robotics, 3D printing, contactless payments, e-commerce, computer stock control, websites;
  Unit 4.3 sustainable/environmental production, eco-packaging, renewable energy, recycling, waste reduction;
  Unit 4.5 Quality Control (inspection) vs Quality Assurance (prevention), quality circles, TQM (as QA context), defect rates, waste;
  location decisions, capacity, just-in-time? (avoid complex), batch/flow/job production.
Marketing (situated in Units 1/2 for Paper 2 context per 2027–29 coursework)
  market research (primary/secondary, surveys, questionnaires, focus groups, observation), segmentation, target market,
  product lifecycle, promotion (advertising: TV/radio/print/social media/influencers, sponsorship), distribution channels, pricing basics.
General study skills / assessment rules (2027–29 Paper 2)
  applied knowledge, application, analysis, evaluation; levels of response mark schemes;
  command words: state, identify, outline, explain, describe, analyse, discuss, evaluate, justify, recommend, calculate.

## BLACKLIST (OUT OF SYLLABUS — reject on sight)
- Ratio analysis beyond the five permitted ratios: stock turnover ratio, debtor days/receivable days, creditor days/payable days, gearing ratio, interest cover, quick ratio is allowed (= acid test), ROCE allowed.
- Full/absorption costing, marginal costing, contribution costing detail beyond break-even basics, standard costing, variance analysis, activity-based costing (ABC).
- Discounted cash flow: NPV, IRR, payback period, discounting, ARR (average rate of return) — even ARR is excluded for Paper 2.
- Working capital cycle / cash operating cycle calculations, liquidity beyond current & acid test.
- Strategy frameworks: SWOT, PESTLE/PEST, Porter's Five Forces, Boston (BCG) matrix, Ansoff matrix, value chain analysis.
- Micro/macro economic theory drills: demand & supply curves, elasticity coefficients (PED/YED), market equilibrium diagrams,
  inflation measurement indexes beyond contextual mention, national income accounting, circular flow, opportunity cost as a graded term-of-art beyond everyday use.
- Market structures theory (perfect competition/monopoly/oligopoly theory), contestable markets.
- Management accounting reports: cost-volume-profit beyond break-even, budgetary control variance reports.
- University-level HR: psychometrics litigation, job evaluation points systems, employment law casework (ACAS/Tribunals named), equal pay acts named.
- Accounting standards & IFRS, depreciation methods (straight line/reducing balance), final accounts formats with balancing figures, cash-flow statement detail (IAS 7).
- Corporate finance: WACC, CAPM, dividend valuation models, gearing theory.
- Forecasting techniques: moving averages, extrapolation of trends, regression.
- Capital budgeting terminology (hurdle rate), opportunity cost deep theory.
- Ethics jargon beyond basic CSR/social responsibility, business ethics PR slogans without evaluation (CSR is allowed topically).

## PAPER 2 ASSESSMENT RULES (2027–2029)
1. Paper 2 = case study with one insert (~3 pages). All questions must apply to THE SAME named business.
2. ZERO pure-knowledge definition questions anywhere in the paper.
3. Part (a) questions (8 marks): applied business context named in the case; two parts of content.
4. Part (b) questions (12 marks):
   - 1(b) and 2(b) MUST use: `Consider [options]. Which … should [Business] choose? Justify your recommendation/decision/answer.` The model answer MUST discuss every option in context, conclude with a justified choice, and explain why rejected options lose.
   - 3(b) MUST use: `Using Appendix 2, evaluate … Has/Does … improved/worsened? Justify your answer.` (schema-fixed; NO “Consider …” prefix). If the question text adds a sub-question (e.g. “Which of the two ratios is the stronger evidence?”), the model answer MUST answer it explicitly.
   - 4(b) MUST use: `Using Appendix 3, evaluate how [Factor 1], [Factor 2] and [Factor 3] will affect [Business]. Which change will have the greatest impact? Justify your answer.` (schema-fixed; NO “Consider …” prefix). Depth rule identical: all three factors in context + justified greatest-impact judgement.
5. 3(a): calculation with sub-parts (i) 2m, (ii) 2m, (iii) 4m.
6. 4(b) question names exactly three external changes with figures from Appendix 3.
7. Stimulus consistency: no fact may appear in questions/answers that contradicts the insert; every figure quoted must originate in the insert or be shown as an explicit computation FROM insert figures.
8. **No unstated assumptions, ever.** Invented component decompositions of variable cost (e.g. “if energy is roughly a quarter of variable cost”, “the $2.60 food component”), invented headcounts of workers affected, unstated hours (2,000h), or assumed base-pay-equals-minimum are REJECTED. Correct forms: (a) figures directly from the insert; (b) values computed from insert data with the arithmetic SHOWN inline (e.g. “0.75% × $500,000 loan = $3,750 a year”; “90,000 units × $0.50 = $45,000 a year”); (c) explicitly labelled illustrative bounds built from insert figures; (d) qualitative judgement when no insert data supports quantification.

## ORCHESTRATOR RULING (2026-11-19)
The 2027–2029 schema (the non-deviatable artifact) fixes 3(b) and 4(b) question text patterns; Tier 2A item 2's “every part (b)” wording is read as applying to 1(b) and 2(b) ONLY. Auditors and generators MUST NOT require a “Consider …” prefix in 3(b)/4(b). Any other deviation (invented facts, blacklist, contradiction, unanswered sub-question) remains an automatic REJECT.
