ENGLISH STUDY DESK — REVISION NOTES

0475 · LITERATURE IN ENGLISH
Read the question for its precise focus. Plan an interpretation and choose evidence
from more than one relevant moment where appropriate. Analyse individual words,
patterns, structural contrasts, stage directions and how they shape a response.
Link each paragraph back to the question.
Check your exam year, text list, entry route and permitted materials with your school.

0500 · FIRST LANGUAGE ENGLISH
Reading: separate explicit details from inferences. Support an inference with
a clue. For summary, group relevant points and paraphrase accurately.
Directed writing: identify purpose, form and audience, evaluate source ideas,
and make the response sound like something that reader would actually receive.
Composition: develop atmosphere through selected details or shape a narrative
around a decision with consequences.

EDITING CHECK
Check full stops, comma splices, subject–verb agreement and consistency of tense.
