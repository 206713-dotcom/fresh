ENGLISH STUDY DESK — 0475 LITERATURE REVISION NOTES

SET TEXTS
Read the question for its precise focus. Plan an interpretation and choose evidence
from more than one relevant moment where appropriate. Explain how the writer’s
language, structure or perspective shapes the reader’s response.

POETRY AND UNSEEN TEXTS
Identify the speaker or narrator, the situation and the initial tone.
Look for changes in imagery, pace, sound or perspective. Build an interpretation
from details across the text.

DRAMA
Imagine the performance. Explain how a pause, gesture, prop or change in
position affects the way an audience reads the dialogue.

EXAM PREPARATION
Check your exam year, set texts, entry route and permitted materials with your school.
Practise under timed conditions and compare your response with the matching mark scheme.
