ENGLISH STUDY DESK — ORIGINAL TIMED PRACTICE

0475 · LITERATURE (15 minutes)
Choose a moment from your set text where a character changes their mind.
Write one analytical paragraph on how the writer makes this change convincing.
Include at least one short quotation and explain one deliberate choice.

0500 · COMPREHENSION (10 minutes)
The evening bus left ten minutes early. A handwritten notice beside the stop
announced a temporary diversion, but the rain had blurred the street name.
Arun looked once at his watch, folded the notice into his pocket and started walking.
Identify two difficulties Arun faced. Then infer how he responded to uncertainty.

0500 · DIRECTED WRITING (20 minutes)
You represent students whose late bus often leaves before clubs finish.
One teacher argues clubs can end earlier. A student survey of a single club
suggests a later bus would help. Write a letter proposing a trial. Evaluate
the strengths and limitations of both ideas and make a realistic recommendation.

GRAMMAR (5 minutes)
Correct the boundaries and agreement in the following sentence.
"The box of exercise books were wet, we moved it away from the window."
Check: "The box of exercise books was wet, so we moved it away from the window."
