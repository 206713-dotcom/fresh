ENGLISH STUDY DESK — ORIGINAL 0475 TIMED PRACTICE

SET-TEXT ESSAY (15 minutes)
Choose a moment from your set text where a character changes their mind.
Write one analytical paragraph on how the writer makes this change convincing.
Include a short quotation and explain one deliberate choice.

UNSEEN POETRY (15 minutes)
    Above the market, voices spill
    from stall to stall like rain.
    One shutter holds the morning light.
    Its owner never came.

How does the poem move from activity to absence? Consider the simile,
the narrowing focus and the effect of the final line.

DRAMA (15 minutes)
    ALI: You can take the letter.
    [Ali steps back, keeping the letter behind his coat.]
    RAE: Then why have you folded it?

How do dialogue and stage directions build tension here? Explain what the
audience can see and how that changes its understanding of the offer.
