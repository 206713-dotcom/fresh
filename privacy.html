About Fresh

Fresh is an independent student project for IGCSE revision. Students and teachers can open the learning tools without creating an account or entering a school email, student number or password.

Student work and browser storage

  • Business Studies. The browser can keep answer drafts, submissions and marks, lesson and checklist progress, missed questions and theme settings.
  • Economics. The browser can keep practice answers and feedback, mock-exam work including saved diagrams, checklist progress, the last card in each flashcard deck and game progress.
  • PyQuest. The browser can keep Python drafts, lesson and quiz progress, level-test results and game progress. The Code Lab runs your Python in your browser.
  • GIS Points. A single balance, recent activity and records of completed rewards are saved in this browser across all Fresh subjects. These are in-site revision rewards.
  • MathQuest, Vertex Maths and RM Revise. The browser can keep practice results, lesson progress and revision settings. MathQuest and RM Revise also keep local game progress.

Fresh uses this information to restore your work, show progress and offer review activities. It stays in the same browser profile. Fresh has no current Google Sheets sync or central store for student submissions.

Most saved work remains after closing the tab. Clearing site data, using private browsing or switching browser profiles can remove or separate it. People who share your browser profile may be able to see it.

AI marking

Business Studies and Economics run their AI marking in the browser. Text and any diagrams used for marking are processed on the device. Model and software files may download from external hosts and be cached in the browser, so the first use may need an internet connection. The download requests fetch files and do not contain your answer.

AI feedback is for practice. Check the mark scheme before relying on a mark or model answer.

Download or remove saved work

Open My progress in either subject and choose Export progress CSV. The spreadsheet-readable file can include drafts, submitted answers, marks, feedback and other progress. Fresh creates the file in your browser. If you send it to a teacher or open it in a cloud spreadsheet, that sharing happens separately.

In PyQuest, open Local profile to download or restore a backup. It can include progress, code drafts and game data. The file is created and read in your browser.

MathQuest and Vertex Maths provide local backup tools. RM Revise can encode your progress in a personal sync link. The shared GIS Points balance stays in the current browser when you export or restore a subject backup. Share a backup or sync link only with someone you want to see your progress.

To remove saved data, first export any work you want to keep. Then open your browser’s site data settings, find gisfresh.me and clear its site data. This can clear progress, preferences and cached files for Fresh’s apps. Downloads and copies you have shared must be managed separately.

Hosting and outside services

When your browser requests a page or file, the service receiving the request can see connection information such as your IP address. Each provider manages its own logs and data retention. Fresh does not install analytics or advertising trackers.

  • Hosting. GitHub Pages serves the website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. GitHub Pages data collection
  • Fonts and sounds. Business Studies loads fonts from Google. Its optional study sounds can also connect to Google. Google privacy policy
  • Model files. The AI tools can request files and software from Hugging Face, GitHub and jsDelivr. Hugging Face privacy policy · GitHub privacy statement · jsDelivr privacy policy
  • Reference links and feedback. External references and video players can receive connection information when you open them. Vertex Maths loads a YouTube player when you choose to play a video. If RM Revise cannot load its local 3D library, it may fetch a copy from cdnjs. The optional Business Studies feedback form opens Google Forms. If you submit feedback, the form owner receives your response and any information the form says it collects. Leave names, school details and student work out of feedback. Google privacy policy

These services may process connection or form data outside your country. Their privacy notices explain where they process information and how long they keep it.

For students, parents and teachers

Use the revision tools for practice answers and diagrams. Keep names, school email addresses, student numbers and identifiable class records out of answers and uploads. On shared devices, follow your school’s guidance and clear your saved data when you finish if other people use the same browser profile.

Earlier versions of Business Studies offered an optional sign-in and saved progress to a Google Sheet. The current app has removed that connection. If you used the earlier version, your teacher can help you contact the Sheet owner about any saved records.

Questions and requests

For a privacy question or a request about previously shared information, ask your teacher to put you in touch with the Fresh team. The team cannot retrieve work stored only in your browser. Use GitHub for general project questions and keep personal information out of public issues. Fresh project on GitHub

Fresh data handling at a glance
AreaHow Fresh works
AccountsStudents and teachers can use the site without an account. The app has no sign-in form or student roster.
Study recordsDrafts, answers, marks and progress are stored in browser storage on gisfresh.me. Fresh does not send them to a student database or school dashboard.
AI markingMarking uses browser workers. A student’s answer and any diagram are passed to a worker on the device. Model downloads use ordinary network requests to get the software and weights.
Local exportsBusiness Studies and Economics generate progress CSVs. PyQuest, MathQuest and Vertex Maths generate backup files for local restore. RM Revise uses personal sync links. Students choose when to export and whether to share the file.
Hosting and resourcesGitHub Pages hosts the website. Google supplies Business Studies fonts and optional sounds. GitHub, Hugging Face and jsDelivr may supply files for the AI tools. Providers receive connection information when these files are requested.
Analytics and storageFresh uses no analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies. The current study tools use localStorage for progress and settings. The Business app migrates old progress cookies where possible and clears former sign-in values.
External formsThe Business feedback link opens a separate Google Form. Form submissions go to its owner. Clicking a link to another site falls under that service’s privacy notice.
Retention and removalStudents or school IT can clear study data through the browser’s site data settings. Downloaded CSVs, old Google Sheets records and logs held by external providers follow their own retention arrangements.

Using Fresh in class

  • Share the subject link or a link to a specific tool. Students can start without supplying identifiers.
  • If you ask students to export work, tell them which school channel to use and who can see the file.
  • On shared devices, reset the browser profile or clear site data when required by your school’s shared-device policy.
  • For review or troubleshooting, use a sample answer that has no real student details.
