Privacy notice

Fresh is an independent student revision site. You can start studying without an account. Your answers and progress stay in the browser you use.

What Fresh saves

Business Studies and Economics save things like practice drafts, submitted answers, feedback, checklist progress, flashcard positions or game progress on this device. Optional AI marking processes answers and any diagrams you choose to attach in your browser. Fresh asks for no student number, school email or password and uploads no study progress to a shared database.

Download or clear your progress

Choose Export progress CSV under My progress to download a spreadsheet-readable copy. It can contain your draft or submitted answers and feedback. The file stays wherever you save it, and you choose who receives a copy.

Progress usually stays until you clear your browser’s site data or your browser removes it. People using the same browser profile may see saved work. To clear it, open your browser’s site data settings, search for gisfresh.me, and remove its data. This clears saved progress, preferences and cached files for both subjects. Downloads and copies you have shared remain separate.

Services your browser connects to

When your browser requests a page or file, the service receiving the request can see connection information such as your IP address.

  • Website hosting. GitHub Pages delivers Fresh and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. GitHub Pages data collection
  • Fonts and audio. Business Studies loads fonts from Google. Its optional study sounds can also load from Google. Google privacy policy
  • AI model files. When you use AI features, your browser can download model files and software from services such as Hugging Face, GitHub and jsDelivr. These requests fetch files so marking can run on your device. Hugging Face privacy policy · jsDelivr privacy policy
  • Links and feedback. Links to revision resources open other sites. The optional Business Studies feedback form opens Google Forms. If you submit it, your answers go to the form owner. Leave student names, numbers and schoolwork out of feedback. Google privacy policy

Questions about privacy

If you use Fresh at school, ask your teacher to put you in touch with the Fresh team. Fresh cannot retrieve answers or progress stored only on your device. Fresh project on GitHub
