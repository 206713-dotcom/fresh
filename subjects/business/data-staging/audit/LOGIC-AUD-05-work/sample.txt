======== P1_U4_MCQ_320 | 4.4 Costs, scale of production and break-even analysis
Q: A manufacturer is comparing two factory locations for producing 10,000 units per year: Location A has annual fixed costs of $50,000 and variable costs of $6 per unit, while Location B has annual fixed costs of $80,000 and variable costs of $2 per unit.   A: Location B, because its total annual cost is $100,000 compared with $110,000 at Location A
  B: Location A, because its fixed costs are $30,000 lower
  C: Location A, because its total annual cost is $60,000
  D: Location B, because its total annual cost is $80,000
KEY: A => Location B, because its total annual cost is $100,000 compared with $110,000 at Location A
CR: Option A is correct because Location A's total cost is $50,000 + (10,000 x $6) = $110,000, whereas Location B's total cost is $80,000 + (10,000 x $2) = $100,000, saving $10,000 per year.
