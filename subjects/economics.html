TOPIC 01

Opportunity cost

Opportunity cost is the value of the next-best alternative given up when you make a choice.

Quick practice

A student can use a free hour for maths practice, football or reading. They choose maths and would otherwise choose football. What is the opportunity cost?

Reveal answer

The benefit they would have gained from playing football.

TOPIC 02

Demand and supply

Equilibrium is the price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied. A shift in either curve can change it.

Quick practice

If demand rises and supply is unchanged, what usually happens to equilibrium price and quantity?

Reveal answer

Both rise under the usual upward-sloping supply and downward-sloping demand assumptions.

TOPIC 03

Inflation

Inflation is a sustained rise in the general price level. It reduces purchasing power if nominal income stays fixed.

Quick practice

Why might someone on a fixed income buy less when prices rise?

Reveal answer

Their income buys fewer goods and services because each unit of money has less purchasing power.
