TOPIC 01

Population change

Natural increase is the birth rate minus the death rate. Migration also affects total population change.

Quick practice

A country has 18 births and 7 deaths per 1,000 people per year. Find its rate of natural increase.

Reveal answer

18 − 7 = 11 per 1,000 people per year, equivalent to 1.1% per year.

Written practice

Explain how a country’s population can shrink even if its birth rate is higher than its death rate.

Compare with the example

More people may leave than arrive. If this net loss from migration is greater than the natural increase, the total population will fall.

TOPIC 02

River processes

Erosion wears material away. Transportation moves it. Deposition occurs when a river can no longer carry some of its load.

Quick practice

Why does sediment often build up on the inside of a meander?

Reveal answer

The current is usually slower on the inside bend, so sediment is more likely to be deposited there.

Written practice

Compare what happens to the banks on the outside and inside of a meander.

Compare with the example

Faster water on the outside tends to erode the bank. Slower water on the inside has less energy to carry sediment, so it deposits material and builds a slip-off slope.

TOPIC 03

Map scale

A representative fraction expresses the ratio of map distance to ground distance in the same units.

Quick practice

At 1:50,000, what ground distance does 4 cm represent?

Reveal answer

4 × 50,000 = 200,000 cm, which is 2 km.

Written practice

Two towns are 6 cm apart on a 1:50,000 map. Show the conversion to kilometres and explain what that distance represents.

Compare with the example

Six centimetres represents 300,000 cm, or 3 km. It gives the straight-line distance on the map. The distance along a winding road can be longer.
