TOPIC 01

Population change

Natural increase is the birth rate minus the death rate. Migration also affects total population change.

Quick practice

A country has 18 births and 7 deaths per 1,000 people per year. Find its rate of natural increase.

Reveal answer

18 − 7 = 11 per 1,000 people per year, equivalent to 1.1% per year.

TOPIC 02

River processes

Erosion wears material away. Transportation moves it. Deposition occurs when a river can no longer carry some of its load.

Quick practice

Why does sediment often build up on the inside of a meander?

Reveal answer

The current is usually slower on the inside bend, so sediment is more likely to be deposited there.

TOPIC 03

Map scale

A representative fraction expresses the ratio of map distance to ground distance in the same units.

Quick practice

At 1:50,000, what ground distance does 4 cm represent?

Reveal answer

4 × 50,000 = 200,000 cm, which is 2 km.
