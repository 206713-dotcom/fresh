Q: [4] Describe how to prepare pure, dry crystals of copper(II) sulfate from copper(II) oxide and dilute sulfuric acid.
+ add excess copper(II) oxide to warm acid :: excess
+ filter to remove unreacted copper(II) oxide :: filter
+ heat / evaporate the filtrate to the point of crystallisation, then leave to cool :: evaporat|heat|crystallisation point|saturated|cool
+ filter off and dry the crystals (between filter paper) :: dry|filter paper|blot
A: Warm the dilute sulfuric acid and add copper(II) oxide until no more dissolves, so it is in excess. Filter to remove the excess oxide. Heat the filtrate to evaporate some water until it reaches the point of crystallisation, then leave it to cool so crystals form. Filter off the crystals, wash with a little cold water and dry them between filter papers.
Q: [3] Describe how to make a pure, dry sample of insoluble barium sulfate.
+ mix solutions of a soluble barium salt and a soluble sulfate :: barium chloride|barium nitrate & sulfate|sulfuric
+ filter to collect the precipitate :: filter
+ wash with distilled water and dry :: wash & dry|dry
A: Mix aqueous barium chloride with aqueous sodium sulfate (or dilute sulfuric acid). A white precipitate of barium sulfate forms. Filter to collect it, wash it with distilled water, and leave it to dry in a warm oven.
