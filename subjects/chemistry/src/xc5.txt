Q: [3] Describe the changes in arrangement and motion of particles when a gas is cooled until it becomes a solid.
+ particles slow down / lose kinetic energy :: slow|lose energy|less energy|less kinetic
+ gas condenses to a liquid: particles come closer and touch :: condens|closer|touch
+ liquid freezes: particles take fixed positions in a regular arrangement and only vibrate :: freez|fixed|regular|vibrat

A: The gas particles are always moving randomly. They collide with the walls of the container, and each collision exerts a force on the walls; this force per unit area is the pressure.
Q: [2] Explain why the boiling point of water is higher than its melting point in terms of the energy needed.
+ boiling needs particles to separate completely / be far apart :: far apart|separate|escape|completely

A: Melting only needs enough energy for the particles to move around each other, while boiling needs enough energy to overcome the forces of attraction completely so the particles separate. This needs more energy, so a higher temperature is required.

Q: [2] Explain what is meant by the limiting reactant.
+ the reactant that is completely used up :: used up|runs out|completely react
+ it determines / limits the amount of product :: amount of product|limits|determines|maximum
A: The limiting reactant is the reactant that is completely used up first; it determines the maximum amount of product that can form. The other reactant is in excess.
