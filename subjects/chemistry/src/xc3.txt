Q: [3] Explain how increased carbon dioxide in the atmosphere leads to climate change.
+ carbon dioxide is a greenhouse gas :: greenhouse
+ absorbs / traps thermal energy (infrared) radiated from Earth :: absorb|trap|infrared|thermal|heat
+ and re-emits it, so the Earth's average temperature rises :: temperature|warm|re-emit|reemit
A: Carbon dioxide is a greenhouse gas. It absorbs some of the thermal (infrared) energy radiated from the Earth's surface and re-emits it, so less escapes to space. This increases the Earth's average temperature, leading to climate change.
Q: [3] Describe how sulfur dioxide is formed and the problems it causes.
+ formed by burning fossil fuels containing sulfur :: fossil|fuel|coal & sulfur|burn
+ dissolves in rain to form acid rain :: acid rain
+ acid rain damages buildings / kills trees / harms aquatic life :: building|limestone|tree|forest|fish|lake|corrod
A: Sulfur dioxide forms when fossil fuels containing sulfur compounds are burned. It dissolves in rainwater to form acid rain, which damages limestone buildings, kills trees and harms life in lakes.
Q: [2] Describe two strategies to reduce the effects of climate change.
+ plant trees / reduce deforestation :: plant|tree|deforest
+ use renewable energy / reduce fossil fuels / hydrogen :: renewable|solar|wind|less fossil|reduce|hydrogen|nuclear
A: Plant more trees and stop deforestation, and use renewable energy sources such as solar and wind instead of burning fossil fuels.
