Q: [4] Describe the mechanism for the reaction of ethanal with HCN in the presence of KCN.
+ CN⁻ lone pair attacks the δ+ carbon of C=O :: cn- & attack|lone pair & δ|carbon
+ the π bond breaks; electrons move to oxygen forming O⁻ (intermediate) :: π|o-|alkoxide|intermediate
+ O⁻ takes H⁺ from HCN, regenerating CN⁻ :: h+|hcn & regenerat|cn-
+ product: 2-hydroxypropanenitrile :: 2-hydroxypropanenitrile
A: The C=O bond is polar, with carbon δ+. A lone pair on CN⁻ attacks the δ+ carbon, and the π electrons of C=O move onto oxygen, forming a negatively charged intermediate. The O⁻ then takes H⁺ from HCN, regenerating CN⁻ (the catalyst). The product is 2-hydroxypropanenitrile, CH₃CH(OH)CN.
