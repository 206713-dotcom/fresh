Q: [3] Describe how a catalytic converter reduces air pollution from cars.
+ converts carbon monoxide to carbon dioxide :: carbon monoxide & carbon dioxide
+ converts oxides of nitrogen to nitrogen :: nitrogen oxide|oxides of nitrogen|no & nitrogen
+ uses a metal catalyst (platinum / rhodium) / equation 2CO + 2NO → 2CO₂ + N₂ :: platinum|rhodium|palladium|2co + 2no
A: Exhaust gases pass over a catalyst of platinum or rhodium. Carbon monoxide is oxidised to carbon dioxide, and oxides of nitrogen are reduced to nitrogen, for example 2CO + 2NO → 2CO₂ + N₂.
