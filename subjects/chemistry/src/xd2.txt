Q: [3] Compare the structures of diamond and graphite and explain why only graphite conducts electricity.
+ diamond: each C bonded to 4 others in a 3D tetrahedral lattice :: diamond & four|4
+ graphite: each C bonded to 3 others in hexagonal layers :: graphite & three|3|layer|hexagon
+ graphite has one delocalised electron per carbon that can move; diamond has none :: delocalised
A: In diamond, each carbon is covalently bonded to four others in a rigid 3D tetrahedral lattice, so all its outer electrons are used in bonds. In graphite, each carbon is bonded to three others in flat hexagonal layers, and the fourth electron is delocalised over the layer and can move, so graphite conducts.
