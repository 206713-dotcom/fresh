TOPIC 01

Osmosis

Water moves through a partially permeable membrane from higher water potential to lower water potential.

Quick practice

Why might potato pieces gain mass in distilled water?

Reveal answer

The water outside has higher water potential than the cell contents. Water enters the cells by osmosis, increasing mass.

TOPIC 02

Photosynthesis

Plants use light energy to make sugars from carbon dioxide and water. Oxygen is released.

Quick practice

Why might brighter light stop increasing the photosynthesis rate?

Reveal answer

A different factor, such as carbon dioxide concentration or temperature, may limit the rate.

TOPIC 03

Inheritance

A dominant allele can affect the phenotype in a heterozygous individual. A Punnett square shows possible offspring genotypes.

Quick practice

For Aa × Aa, what fraction of offspring are expected to have genotype aa?

Reveal answer

The possible combinations are AA, Aa, Aa and aa. The expected fraction is 1/4.
