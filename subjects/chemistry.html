TOPIC 01

Ionic bonding

An ionic bond is the electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions. Ionic compounds form giant lattices.

Quick practice

Why does molten sodium chloride conduct electricity?

Reveal answer

Its ions are free to move and carry charge through the liquid.

TOPIC 02

Balancing equations

Change coefficients to conserve the number of atoms of each element. Leave chemical formulae intact.

Quick practice

Balance Mg + O₂ → MgO.

Reveal answer

2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO. Both sides have two magnesium and two oxygen atoms.

TOPIC 03

Rates of reaction

A higher temperature increases particle kinetic energy and the fraction of collisions with energy at or above the activation energy.

Quick practice

Why does powdered calcium carbonate react faster with acid than the same mass of large chips?

Reveal answer

The powder exposes more surface area to the acid, increasing the frequency of collisions.
