TOPIC 01

Linear equations

Use inverse operations on both sides to isolate the unknown. Substitute your answer back into the original equation to check it.

Quick practice

Solve 3x + 7 = 22.

Reveal answer

Subtract 7 to get 3x = 15. Divide by 3 to get x = 5.

TOPIC 02

Right-angled trigonometry

Relative to the chosen angle, sine is opposite ÷ hypotenuse, cosine is adjacent ÷ hypotenuse and tangent is opposite ÷ adjacent.

Quick practice

The side opposite θ is 6 cm and the adjacent side is 8 cm. Find θ to one decimal place.

Reveal answer

tan θ = 6 ÷ 8 = 0.75, so θ = tan⁻¹(0.75) ≈ 36.9°.

TOPIC 03

Probability

For equally likely outcomes, divide the number of favourable outcomes by the total number of outcomes.

Quick practice

A bag holds 4 red and 6 blue counters. Find the probability of drawing a red counter at random.

Reveal answer

P(red) = 4 ÷ 10 = 2/5.
