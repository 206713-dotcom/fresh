TOPIC 01

Motion

The gradient of a speed–time graph gives the rate of change of speed. The area under it gives distance travelled.

Quick practice

A cyclist travels at a steady 6 m/s for 12 s. Find the distance.

Reveal answer

Distance = speed × time = 6 × 12 = 72 m.

TOPIC 02

Forces

For a constant mass, the resultant force equals mass × acceleration. Use kilograms, metres per second squared and newtons.

Quick practice

A 4 kg object accelerates at 3 m/s². Find the resultant force.

Reveal answer

F = ma = 4 × 3 = 12 N in the direction of acceleration.

TOPIC 03

Circuits

Potential difference equals current × resistance. Components in series carry the same current.

Quick practice

A 6 Ω resistor has a 12 V potential difference across it. Find the current.

Reveal answer

I = V ÷ R = 12 ÷ 6 = 2 A.
