TOPIC 01

Inference

An inference is a conclusion supported by clues. Identify the detail and explain how it supports your interpretation.

Quick practice

‘Lina reread the message three times before pressing send.’ What might this suggest about Lina?

Reveal answer

She may be nervous about how the message will be received. Rereading it three times suggests she wants to avoid a mistake.

TOPIC 02

Writer’s effects

Pick a precise word or image. Explain what it suggests and how it shapes the reader’s impression of the scene.

Quick practice

What effect does ‘the wind clawed at the shutters’ create?

Reveal answer

‘Clawed’ makes the wind sound like an attacking animal, giving the scene a threatening atmosphere.

TOPIC 03

Summary writing

Select points that answer the question, combine related ideas and express them concisely in your own words.

Quick practice

Summarise in one sentence: ‘The hall leaked. Several windows were broken. Members had stopped attending because the room was often cold.’

Reveal answer

Attendance fell because the poorly maintained hall was cold.
