Coursework and speaking routes

Component 3 can replace Paper 2. The coursework portfolio includes a source-based argumentative, discursive or persuasive assignment (30 marks), description (25) and narrative (25). Optional Component 4 is a separately endorsed individual talk and conversation worth 40 marks.

    Revision library

    Common marking priorities

    Inference

    Use a detail as evidence and explain the conclusion it supports. Repeatedly checking a message before sending it can suggest concern about how it will be received.

    “Lina reread the message three times before pressing send.” What might this suggest about Lina?

    Reveal example answer

    Lina may be nervous about how the message will be received. Reading it three times implies she wants to avoid a mistake.

    Writer’s effects

    Explain what a precise choice means in context, then what it suggests. In “the wind clawed at the shutters”, “clawed” evokes an animal trying to break in.

    What effect does “the wind clawed at the shutters” create?

    Reveal example answer

    “Clawed” makes the wind seem like an animal attacking the shutters, making the scene threatening.

    Selective summary

    Group related points and use continuous prose. Combine a leaky roof and broken windows into a single point about poor maintenance if the task asks why attendance fell.

    Summarise why attendance fell. “The hall leaked. Several windows were broken. Members had stopped attending because the room was often cold.”

    Reveal example answer

    Attendance fell because the poorly maintained hall was often cold.

    Source evaluation

    Consider the strengths and limitations of each view. Explain why visitor totals alone may give the council a poor measure of the impact on neighbours.

    Description

    Move attention between telling details. A clock, a wet footprint and a light going out can establish a place and a change in atmosphere.

    Narrative

    Build the story around a consequential decision. Develop what the character wants, what stands in their way and what their choice changes.
