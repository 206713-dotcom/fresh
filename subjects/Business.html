TOPIC 01

Break-even

At break-even, total revenue equals total costs. Contribution per unit is selling price minus variable cost per unit.

Quick practice

Fixed costs are $600. Each item sells for $8 and costs $5 in variable costs. Find break-even output.

Reveal answer

Contribution is $3 per item. Break-even output is $600 ÷ $3 = 200 items.

TOPIC 02

Market research

Primary research gathers new data for a specific decision. Secondary research uses data that already exists.

Quick practice

A bakery surveys students about preferred snacks. What type of research is this?

Reveal answer

Primary research, because the bakery collects new responses for its own decision.

TOPIC 03

Cash flow

Net cash flow is inflows minus outflows. A business can run short of cash when customers pay after expenses are due.

Quick practice

A shop opens the month with $300, receives $900 and pays $1,000. Find its closing cash balance.

Reveal answer

Net cash flow is −$100. Closing balance is $300 − $100 = $200.
