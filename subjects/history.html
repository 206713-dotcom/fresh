TOPIC 01

Causes

Distinguish long-term conditions from short-term triggers. Explain how each factor contributed to the outcome.

Quick practice

An alliance system existed for years before a crisis led to war. How could the alliances have contributed?

Reveal answer

Alliance commitments could widen a local conflict by drawing in countries that supported the original participants.

TOPIC 02

Source evaluation

Use a source’s content, origin, audience and purpose to judge how useful it is for a specific question.

Quick practice

What can a wartime recruitment poster tell a historian about recruitment?

Reveal answer

It shows the arguments and images used to persuade people. Actual enlistment figures would help assess how recruitment changed.

TOPIC 03

Comparing explanations

Support each explanation with evidence and use a clear criterion, such as impact or timing, to justify your judgement.

Quick practice

What makes an explanation of a policy’s failure stronger?

Reveal answer

It connects specific evidence to the outcome and explains why the factor mattered compared with other causes.
