TOPIC 01

Imagery

Explain what an image makes you picture and how it changes your reading of a character, setting or theme.

Quick practice

What does ‘a cage of golden bars’ suggest about a wealthy character’s home?

Reveal answer

‘Golden’ suggests luxury, while ‘cage’ suggests confinement. The image implies that the character can be privileged and trapped at the same time.

TOPIC 02

Structure

Track shifts in tone, point of view or pace. Connect a shift to its effect on the reader.

Quick practice

A poem moves from ‘we’ to ‘I’ in its last stanza. Suggest one possible effect.

Reveal answer

The shift narrows the focus from a group to one speaker and may highlight isolation.

TOPIC 03

Drama

Stage directions guide performance. Consider how a pause, gesture or movement changes the audience’s interpretation.

Quick practice

A character says ‘Of course’ after a long pause. How might the pause change the line?

Reveal answer

The delay can suggest reluctance or doubt, making an apparent agreement feel uncertain.
