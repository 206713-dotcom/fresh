How to earn GIS Points

Earn across all 12 subjects with one balance saved in this browser. A correct quiz answer usually earns 3 points, a full lesson or reviewed written response earns 10, and longer challenges pay up to 20 base points.

Most activities pay once per question, topic or challenge. For daily rewards and gear bonuses, check the details below.
